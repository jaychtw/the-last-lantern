# Revised asset provenance

The active revision uses original Blender-authored 3D assets and articulated pose renders, generated by Codex for this project in `scripts/render-assets.py`. Source meshes/materials/lighting and reusable `.blend` files are inspectable under `assets/source/`. Runtime WebP textures and animation atlases are under `public/art3d/`; camera anchors and raw-export SHA-256 provenance are in `runtime.json`. `scripts/pack-3d-assets.py` encodes and packs the actual rendered images; it does not paint over a concept reference.

The production source includes separate blue/copper slate cottages with timber/gables, individual tiles, masonry, shutters/window boxes, door hardware and lanterns; vegetation, rocks, cobbled terrain, water/banks, bridge, furniture, chests and a Waylantern; original Rowan, Mira and Dewdrop models. Rowan's blue cloak, chestnut hair, teal eyes, leather tunic/boots/satchel, brass lantern and short blade are reused across exploration, portraits and battle. Mira has a silver-haired, plum/apron, brass-spectacled identity. The original leaf-crowned Dewdrop has pearly botanical coloring and lobed feet rather than a franchise monster's silhouette.

These are AI-assisted source-authored assets, not claimed to be human-sculpted commercial production art. No third-party game asset, model library, map, logo, music sample or reused concept-background image is used. The owner's collage informs the broad visual direction, not reusable pixels. No part of it is inserted into the playable game. Scripted geometry/materials and model reuse provide provenance and character consistency; human quality acceptance remains pending.

One original AI-generated distant sky/valley matte (`assets/source/battle-sky.png`) supplies atmospheric distance behind the transparent Blender battle clearing. Its role, production prompt and SHA-256 are recorded in `battle-sky.provenance.json`. It contains no actors, foreground props, buildings, UI or completed gameplay scene. It is an independently authored production layer, not the owner's collage or a substitute for a functioning scene. All near-field architecture/ground/foliage, characters and combat feedback remain separate authored or animated layers. This mixed pipeline has an art-matching risk: the distant matte is more painterly/detailed than the procedural models.

Blender renders use CPU Cycles with authored warm key/cool rim light, procedural material detail, physical geometry and transparent shadow-catching ground. The host lacks OpenImageDenoise, so sampled renders can retain some grain. Source authoring is bounded to the representative Gate A scenes. The runtime is a fixed-camera depth-sorted compositor with independently moving actors; it does not turn a screenshot into a level. No real-time free camera or physically dynamic scene-wide shadows are claimed.

Fraunces and DM Sans are bundled locally under SIL OFL 1.1; complete licenses are in `docs/licenses/` and the review package. Chinese text uses device system fonts. Original short Web Audio oscillator cues remain optional/muted by default; there are no sampled tracks. No accounts, telemetry, external runtime font service or image CDN is needed.

The first vector candidate at commit `4156480a644648e6e92fd8c68193223ff727f79a` was rejected. Its art direction and QA evidence must not be described as approval of this revision. Fresh running-game captures and a candid reference comparison are required in `GATE-A-REVIEW.md`.

## Gate B production extension

The owner approved the revised Gate A direction; see `APPROVALS.md`. `scripts/render-campaign-assets.py` extends the same source geometry/material/light family with original Bramble Hopper, Moss Moth, Pebbleback, Lantern Wisp, Twig Sentinel, Cave Nibbler, Ashling, Hollow Knight, Watchtower Eye, Thornkeeper, Rootbound Warden and Dim Guardian. Each has a separate source model and action presentation. Names follow the GDD's original working concepts; no franchise character design or model is reused.

The extension also authors Arlen, equipment/quest icons, actual carved rune tablets, fern/arch/pillar/crystal props, shrine/forest/cavern/quarry/tower/sanctum grounds and matching region battle scenery. These assets remain separate from Rowan, collision, interactable state, UI and action effects. Runtime textures and raw-source hashes are in the current manifest; reusable models are in `assets/source/`. The original distant matte supplies sky/valley distance only in open-air meadow/quarry battle compositions.

Audio now includes original quiet synthesized pentatonic ambience as well as cues, with no sampled or licensed music. These are AI-assisted authored assets and code, not claimed to be a human art team's commercial production. New campaign scenes still require human gameplay/visual review; initial direction approval does not automatically approve every asset or composition.
